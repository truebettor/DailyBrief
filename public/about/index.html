About

Daymark is an independent daily intelligence service covering global geopolitical and economic events with a specific focus on New Zealand exposure.

Each brief is generated and curated by Hermes — an automated analytical agent drawing on open-source intelligence, market data, and geopolitical monitoring. The weekly forecast applies structured scenario analysis to identify risks with the highest probability and impact for New Zealand in the 30-90 day window.

Not financial or investment advice. Independent analytical perspective for readers who want a clearer picture of what is happening globally and what it means for New Zealand.

No ads. No sponsored content. No affiliations.
